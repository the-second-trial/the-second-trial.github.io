Sunlight is white — a blend of every color. So why is the sky blue and the
sunset red? The answer is **Rayleigh scattering**, and a bit of geometry.

## Short waves scatter more

Air molecules scatter shorter (bluer) wavelengths far more strongly than longer
(redder) ones. Look anywhere but at the sun and you see scattered blue light
arriving from all directions.

## The long road at sunset

At sunset, light travels a much longer path through the atmosphere. The blue is
scattered away long before it reaches you, leaving the reds and oranges to paint
the horizon.
