Sorting is the "hello, world" of algorithm design — simple to state, surprisingly
deep to do well. Watching the classic algorithms run side by side makes their
differences vivid.

## The contenders

- **Bubble sort** — easy to understand, painfully slow.
- **Merge sort** — divide, conquer, and combine; reliably efficient.
- **Quicksort** — fast on average, with a worst case to respect.
- **Heap sort** — turns the array into a heap and drains it in order.

Press start and let them race.
