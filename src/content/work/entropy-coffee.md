Leave a hot coffee on the desk and it cools to room temperature. Wait as long
as you like — it will never spontaneously reheat. That one-way street is the
**second law of thermodynamics** at work.

## Counting the ways

Entropy measures how many microscopic arrangements correspond to what we see.
A hot mug in a cool room is a low-entropy, special situation; spreading the heat
out is overwhelmingly more probable simply because there are vastly more ways to
do it.

The coffee cools not because it is forced to, but because *almost every*
possible future is one in which it already has.
