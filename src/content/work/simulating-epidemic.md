The classic SIR model splits a population into three groups —
**S**usceptible, **I**nfected, and **R**ecovered — and tracks how people move
between them. Three simple equations, and the whole arc of an outbreak appears.

## Levers that matter

- **R0** — how many people each case infects on average.
- **Vaccination** — moving people straight to recovered before they catch it.
- **Timing** — the same intervention has wildly different effects early versus
  late.

Pull the levers in this simulation and watch the curve flatten, peak, or
vanish.
