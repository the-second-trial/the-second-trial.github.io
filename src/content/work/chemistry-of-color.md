Drop a pinch of table salt into a flame and it glows orange. Copper turns it
green. Each element has its own signature color — a fingerprint written in
light.

## Electrons on a ladder

Heat excites electrons to higher energy levels. When they fall back down, they
release the energy as light of a very specific wavelength — and that wavelength
is fixed by the spacing of the element's energy "ladder."

The same physics that colors a flame test lets us read the composition of
distant stars from their spectra.
