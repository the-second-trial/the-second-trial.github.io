A handful of trivial rules, applied to a grid of cells, can produce gliders,
oscillators, and patterns that never repeat. Cellular automata are the purest
demonstration that **complexity does not require complicated rules**.

## Conway's Game of Life

Three rules about neighbors — born, survive, die — and an entire universe of
behavior falls out. Some configurations are stable, some blink forever, and
some crawl across the grid like living things.

This playground lets you draw a starting state and watch where the rules take
it.
