Carbon is constantly on the move — between the atmosphere, the oceans, living
things, and rock. Putting *numbers* on those flows turns a vague diagram into
something you can actually reason about.

## Stocks and flows

The atmosphere holds a relatively small amount of carbon compared to the deep
ocean and sediments — which is exactly why modest extra emissions can shift its
balance so quickly.

Tracking the fluxes, in gigatonnes per year, shows where the budget closes and
where it doesn't.
